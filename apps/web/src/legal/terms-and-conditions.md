## 9. Buyer Protection

9.1. **Overview.** The Mulligans Buyer Protection programme is designed to give buyers confidence when purchasing items through the Platform. It is funded by the 7.5% Buyer Protection fee charged on each transaction.

9.2. **What is covered.** Buyer Protection covers the following situations:
   - (a) **Item not received** — the item has not been delivered within a reasonable timeframe and tracking information indicates it has not arrived or is lost in transit;
   - (b) **Significantly not as described** — the item received is materially different from the listing description, photographs, or specified condition. Examples include (but are not limited to):
     - an item listed as "new" that shows clear signs of use;
     - an item with undisclosed significant defects or damage;
     - an item that is a different brand, model, or specification than listed;
     - an item missing components that were included in the listing;
     - **a counterfeit, replica, or imitation item that was listed as genuine**; and
   - (c) **Damaged in transit** — the item was damaged during shipping and is materially different from its described condition at the time of dispatch.

9.3. **What is not covered.** Buyer Protection does not cover:
   - (a) **Buyer's remorse** — situations where the buyer simply changes their mind about a purchase, or the item is not to their personal preference but matches the listing description;
   - (b) **Minor variations** — minor differences between the item and the listing that do not materially affect the item's value, functionality, or usability (for example, slight colour variations due to photography or screen differences);
   - (c) **Wear consistent with the described condition** — signs of use that are consistent with the condition described in the listing (for example, normal sole wear on a set of irons listed as "used - good condition");
   - (d) **Damage caused by the buyer** — any damage that occurs after the buyer has received and accepted the item;
   - (e) **Items altered by the buyer** — items that have been modified, repaired, or altered by the buyer after receipt;
   - (f) **Off-platform transactions** — transactions conducted outside the Platform, even if the parties initially connected through Mulligans; and
   - (g) **Transactions involving prohibited items** — items that should not have been listed under Section 6.

9.4. **Claim window.** Buyers must raise a Buyer Protection claim within **3 calendar days** from the date of confirmed delivery (as indicated by the carrier's tracking information). If no claim is raised within this period, the transaction will be automatically completed and funds released to the seller. Once funds have been released, Buyer Protection claims can no longer be accepted for that transaction.

9.5. **How to make a claim.** To make a Buyer Protection claim, the buyer must:
   - (a) open a dispute through the Platform's dispute resolution feature;
   - (b) select the applicable reason for the claim (e.g., not as described, not received, counterfeit, damaged);
   - (c) provide a clear written description of the issue;
   - (d) upload photographic evidence showing the problem (where applicable); and
   - (e) specify the refund amount requested.

9.6. **Claim review.** All Buyer Protection claims are reviewed in accordance with the dispute resolution process set out in Section 13. Mulligans will review the evidence provided by both buyer and seller before making a determination.

9.7. **Remedies.** Where a Buyer Protection claim is upheld, the buyer may receive one or more of the following remedies:
   - (a) a full refund of the item price (the Buyer Protection fee, Service fee, and Shipping Protection fee are non-refundable except where required by law — see Section 7.8);
   - (b) a partial refund, where the issue affects only part of the item's value;
   - (c) a return-for-refund, where the buyer returns the item to the seller and receives a refund upon the seller confirming receipt (see Section 14); or
   - (d) any other remedy that Mulligans considers fair and appropriate in the circumstances.
